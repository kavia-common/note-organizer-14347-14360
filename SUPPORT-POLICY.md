# Support Policy

Scope
- Ocean Notes is provided as-is. The core features include creating, editing, organizing, pinning, and deleting notes.

Requests
- Please open an issue with steps to reproduce, expected vs actual behavior, screenshots (if possible), and device details.

Response
- Maintainers review issues on a best-effort basis.
- Security-related reports receive priority attention.

Out of Scope
- Custom feature development or private forks.
- Third-party service integrations not part of the project roadmap.

See also
- APP-USAGE.md for usage, TROUBLESHOOTING-APP.md for common issues,
  and CI-TROUBLESHOOTING.md for native build guidance.
