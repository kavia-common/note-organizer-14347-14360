# Roadmap

Short term
- Rich text/Markdown editing option
- Color tags and quick filters
- Archive folder and bulk actions
- Undo snackbar for delete
- Keyboard shortcuts on web/dev builds

Medium term
- Search by tag and content highlighting
- Bi-directional sync (opt-in) with a backend
- Share sheet improvements (attachments, images)
- Offline-first sync conflict resolution

Long term
- End-to-end encryption for synced data
- Multi-device presence and collaboration
- Theming presets and custom palette editor
