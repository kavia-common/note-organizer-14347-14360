# Privacy Policy (Template)

Ocean Notes is a local-only notes application. By default, it does not transmit your notes or personal data to any server.

Data We Store
- Notes and folders are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.

Data Sharing
- Data is not shared unless you explicitly export or share notes. When exporting or sharing, you control the destination.

Analytics/Telemetry
- None by default. If analytics are added in the future, they will be opt-in.

Security
- Protect your device with a passcode/biometrics. Exported files/clipboard content may contain sensitive data—handle with care.

Contact
- See MAINTAINERS.md for maintainer contact details.
