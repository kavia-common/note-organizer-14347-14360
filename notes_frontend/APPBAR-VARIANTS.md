# App Bar Variants

Default (title + Add):
- Use for main list and editor/view screens.
- Keep actions minimal; Add (+) is the primary.

With Subtitle:
- Use `AppBar.withSubtitle` to show a concise contextual line (e.g., current filter or workspace).
- Keep copy short and muted.

With Shortcut Buttons:
- Use `ShortcutButtons` to show secondary actions like Help/About/Dev.
- Keep to 2–3 actions max to preserve clarity.

Branding:
- A small brand text (e.g., "Ocean Pro") can be placed on the right for home screen only.
