# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Assume positive intent.

- Welcome newcomers and support learning.
- Provide actionable feedback with empathy.
- Avoid harassment or exclusionary behavior.
- Respect differing opinions and approaches.

Reports
- Contact maintainers (see MAINTAINERS.md). Provide context and links/screenshots if possible.

Consequences
- Maintainers may moderate content and contributions to maintain a healthy, productive environment.
